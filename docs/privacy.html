CLEAR, SIMPLE, AND CLOSE TO HOME

Your words.
Your own space.

Stillword does not require an account, show advertising, or include analytics. The app does not send your reflection notes, saved verses, or reading history to a Stillword server.

What stays on your device

Your reading start date, saved verses, reflections, marked reading days, text-size preference, and reminder settings are saved in local app or browser storage. Notifications are scheduled with your device’s operating system. These records do not sync between devices, and there is no cloud backup or account recovery.

Editing and removing your data

Use Edit reflection and save an empty note to delete a reflection. Tap a saved verse’s bookmark to remove the bookmark; a verse with a reflection remains in Saved until its note is removed too. Clearing the app’s storage or browser website data removes local reading information and starts a new reading plan. Uninstalling may leave local files, depending on your platform.

Offline reading and notifications

Android and Windows support local reminders. The iPhone web edition offers offline reading after setup, without scheduled notifications. Browsers may remove offline files under storage pressure. Reopen online to prepare the web app again. Focus settings, device permissions, and sleep can affect notification delivery.

When you use the website

The website and downloads use GitHub Pages and GitHub Releases. Visiting them sends normal web requests to GitHub. External links open services with their own privacy practices. Copying a verse puts its text on your device’s clipboard.

Need a hand?

For a missing notification, open Rhythm and send a test. Check your device’s notification settings and reopen Stillword at least every 20 days to refresh its queue. For another problem, tell us your app version, device type, and what happened. Please keep private reflections out of public reports.

About this edition

Scripture uses the King James Version; source and font licenses are included in the project repository.
