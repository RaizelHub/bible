A LITTLE GRACE AT YOUR DESK

A quieter moment.
Right on your desktop.

Let a fresh verse meet you between the busy moments. The Windows app brings Stillword’s calm reading space, saved verses, and daily reminders to your PC.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 (2004+) / Windows 11 · x64 · v1.2.0 preview · Free

Close the window. Keep your rhythm.

Windows holds your next 20 days of morning, afternoon, and night reminders, even after you close Stillword. Your PC needs to be on and awake. Open the app at least every 20 days to prepare the next set, and after changing timezones.

  1. Download and install

    Open Stillword-Windows-Setup.exe and follow the setup steps. It installs for your Windows account and adds a Start menu shortcut; a desktop shortcut is optional. This preview is unsigned, so Windows may show an unknown publisher notice.

  2. Choose your moments

    Open Stillword, select Rhythm, and enable the moments you want: morning, afternoon, night, or just one daily verse. Each time is adjustable. Reminders begin only after you enable them.

  3. Send a test notification

    In Rhythm, select Send a test notification. If you do not see a banner, open Windows Settings → System → Notifications and allow Stillword. Do not disturb or Focus settings can silence banners.

  4. Make it part of your day

    Read, copy, and save the verses that speak to you. The app works offline and uses each of its 365 readings once, without an automatic restart. Turning a reminder off removes its future alerts. Uninstall through Windows Settings → Apps when needed.

Bookmarks and reading progress stay on this PC and do not sync with your phone. Missed reading slots are skipped. This is the Windows edition; Mac and Linux downloads are not available yet.
