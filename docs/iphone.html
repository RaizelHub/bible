FREE IPHONE HOME SCREEN APP

A little stillness.
Right on your iPhone.

Keep Stillword beside your other apps. Fresh scripture and saved verses, without an App Store download or developer membership.

Reading, wherever you are.

Includes 365 unique KJV readings, bookmarks, and offline access after setup. This web edition does not send scheduled notifications. The Android and Windows downloads include morning, afternoon, and night alerts.

  1. Open Stillword in Safari

    On your iPhone, use Safari to open the app below. If this page is inside another app’s browser, copy the link into Safari first.

    Open iPhone app

  2. Wait for “Ready offline”

    The first visit downloads the app and its readings. Stay online until the status at the top says Ready offline. You can then use it without an internet connection.

  3. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen

    In Safari, open the Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen. Depending on your Safari layout, Share may be inside the More menu.

  4. Add it, and make it yours

    Keep Open as Web App switched on if shown, then tap Add. Open Stillword using its new Home Screen icon. Start your reading plan there and save the verses you want to keep.

Your reading plan and bookmarks stay in this browser or installed app and do not sync between devices. Clearing website data can remove them. If iOS clears offline files to free space, open Stillword online again.
