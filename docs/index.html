A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A little clarity.

So you can settle into your rhythm.

How do I install it on an iPhone?+

Use the iPhone installation guide. Open the web app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It is free and does not need the App Store. This edition offers offline reading and bookmarks, but does not send scheduled notifications.

Will I see the same verse again?+

The new collection assigns 365 distinct verses to successive morning, afternoon, and night slots. It never loops back. At three readings a day, it ends during day 122, and new verse reminders stop. Saved verses remain available. Disabled or missed daily moments are skipped; reinstalling or clearing app data starts a new plan.

Does it work without internet?+

Yes. On iPhone, open the web app online first and wait for Ready offline; scripture and bookmarks are then available without internet. Your browser may clear cached files when storage is low. The Android app also supports offline notifications, preparing up to 20 days at a time. The iPhone web edition has no scheduled notifications.

Can I choose just one reminder a day?+

Of course. Enable only your preferred moment in the Rhythm tab, or keep all three. Each time is editable. The reading calendar keeps moving even when a reminder is turned off.

Why didn’t a notification appear exactly on time?+

Android may deliver reminders a little later to save battery. Your phone’s notification permissions, Do Not Disturb, Focus, and battery settings also affect banners. Use “Send a test notification” in the app to check your settings.

Which Bible translation does Stillword use?+

Stillword uses the King James Version (KJV). Every verse includes its scripture reference. The collection draws from Psalms, Proverbs, the Gospels, and New Testament letters.

Do I need an account?+

No account, no subscription, and no advertising. Your reading plan and saved verses stay on your device. This website does not use analytics or tracking cookies.
